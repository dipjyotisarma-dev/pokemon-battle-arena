Game Rules

Purpose Trainers Teams Moves Combat Matches Scoring Available Pokémon

01 Purpose of the Game

Battle Arena is a competitive simulator: build a six-Pokémon team, fight complete 6-v-6 matches against an AI opponent, and earn points that determine your leaderboard rank.

02 Trainer Registration

Every trainer registers with a public trainer name, an email, and a password. The trainer name is what appears on the leaderboard and in battle.

03 Team Creation

Every trainer needs a complete team before entering the arena.

  • Exactly six Pokémon per team.
  • No duplicate Pokémon within a team.
  • At most one Legendary, Mythical, or Ultra Beast per team — the rest must be standard Pokémon.
  • Each Pokémon must have exactly four moves, with no duplicate moves.

Teams can be edited at any time from the dashboard; the same validation rules apply to edits.

04 Moves, Types & STAB

Every move has a type, a category (Physical or Special), and a base power. Physical moves scale off Attack and Defense; Special moves scale off Special Attack and Special Defense.

STAB (Same-Type Attack Bonus): if a move's type matches one of the user's own types, its damage is multiplied by 1.5×.

Type effectiveness follows the standard chart: a move can be super effective (2×), not very effective (0.5×), or have no effect (0×) depending on the defender's types.

05 Turn Order & Damage

Turn order for each battle is decided by Speed — the faster Pokémon acts first each round, and combat alternates until one Pokémon faints.

damage = ((2 × power × atk / def) / 10) × STAB × typeEffectiveness × randomFactor

Each Pokémon's fighting HP for the match — its Battle HP — is derived from its base HP rather than used directly.

Battle HP = 5 × Base HP

When a Pokémon's Battle HP reaches zero, it faints and cannot be used again for the rest of that arena match.

06 Matches & Exiting

A complete arena match is 6-v-6: each of your six Pokémon battles exactly once against an opponent Pokémon. You may exit an ongoing battle at any time from the Battle screen.

  • Exiting mid-match discards that incomplete match — it is not scored and earns no points.
  • Only fully completed matches count toward your stats and the leaderboard.
  • If you've completed at least one match before exiting, you'll see a full session summary before returning to the dashboard.

07 Scoring & Leaderboard

Completed matches award points based on wins within that match. Your total points, win count, and match count update your leaderboard rank immediately after a completed session. The final battle summary shows the match outcome, points earned, and your updated rank.

08 Available Pokémon

The full roster is browsable in the Pokédex, including base stats, types, and each Pokémon's Battle HP. Legendary, Mythical, and Ultra Beast entries are clearly marked, since only one may appear per team.
