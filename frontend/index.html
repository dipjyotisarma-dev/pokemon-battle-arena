THE COMPETITIVE SYSTEM

Arena Rulebook

01 / THE FORMAT

6-Match Sequential Run

A run consists of 6 sequential 1-v-1 matchups against an AI roster. Each of your 6 Pokémon battles only once per run. When a Pokémon faints or completes its match, it is locked for the remainder of that run.

02 / BUILD YOUR TEAM

Roster & Move Constraints

Teams require exactly 6 unique Pokémon with 4 unique learnable moves each. At most 1 Legendary, Mythical, or Ultra Beast is permitted. The backend authoritatively validates slot order (1–6), duplicate bans, and move legality.

03 / STATS & BATTLE HP

Base HP vs. Battle HP

Base HP is the Pokémon's core database stat. In combat, health scales using the authoritative formula:
Battle HP = (3 × Base HP) + ⌊BST / 2⌋
When Battle HP reaches 0, the Pokémon faints for the rest of the run.

04 / TURN ORDER

Speed Priority & Ties

The faster combatant attacks first each turn (Speed_A > Speed_B). If Speed stats are tied, the first attacker is decided by an unbiased 50/50 coin toss. Turns strictly alternate until a combatant faints.

05 / MOVES & DAMAGE

Categories & Effectiveness

Physical moves use Attack / Defense; Special moves use Sp. Atk / Sp. Def. Effectiveness adds +0.5 per advantage or subtracts -0.5 per disadvantage.
Damage = ⌊Power × (Atk / Def) × Effectiveness⌋ (STAB 1.5× multiplier).

06 / MATCH FLOW

9-Stage Progression

Runs progress through: Team Reveal → Match Intro → Pokémon Selection → Matchup Confirmation → Countdown → Active Combat → Match Summary → Next Match → Session Summary. Exiting mid-match discards that match with 0 points.

07 / SCORING & LEADERBOARD

Damage Ratios & Ranking

Scored on HP efficiency: Base Points = (Opponent Lost % - Trainer Lost %) × 100. Victories earn 10 + Base Points; defeats earn Base Points. The leaderboard ranks trainers by total completed points descending.

08 / HOW TO PLAY

Quickstart Walkthrough

1. Register trainer → 2. Build 6-Pokémon team → 3. Assign 4 moves → 4. Enter Arena → 5. Preview opponent → 6. Select counter → 7. Confirm matchup → 8. Read turn order → 9. Choose moves → 10. Complete 6 matches!
